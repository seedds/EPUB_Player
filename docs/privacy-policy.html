Privacy Policy for EPUB Player

Last updated: May 15, 2026

EPUB Player is an iPhone and iPad app for reading EPUB files, especially EPUB3 books with media overlays. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Information Stored on Your Device

EPUB Player stores the content you import into the app on your device. This may include:

  • EPUB files you import
  • custom font files you import
  • reading progress and reader settings
  • cached cover images and playback-related data needed by the app

This information is used only to provide the app's library, reading, playback, and customization features.

Local Network Access

EPUB Player may request access to your local network so you can upload supported files from another device on the same network. This feature is used only to let you send files such as .epub, .ttf, and .otf to the app.

Local network access is user-initiated and is not used for advertising or tracking.

Data Collection

EPUB Player does not require an account. In the current version, the app is designed to work primarily with files and settings stored locally on your device.

The current version of EPUB Player does not use third-party advertising SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs.

Data Sharing

EPUB Player does not sell your personal information. Your imported files remain under your control on your device, except when you choose to transfer files to the app over your local network.

Third-Party Services

EPUB Player uses open-source reading components to render and play supported EPUB content on-device. If a future version adds analytics, crash reporting, cloud features, or other third-party services, this Privacy Policy may be updated to describe those changes.

Children's Privacy

EPUB Player does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer through the project page: https://github.com/seedds/EPUB_Player
